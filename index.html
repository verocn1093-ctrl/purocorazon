05 Our story

Cooked with puro corazón

Puro Corazón UK launched on 6 July 2021 with one idea: share the flavours of Peru with London.

Led by director Joan Vallejos Bueno, the brand focuses on Peruvian cuisine and on the passion of sharing it.
