the experience

Puro Corazón UK shares Peruvian food and culinary experiences in London. Beef heart anticuchos, grilled potatoes, wings and sauces, made with heart and meant to be shared.

Specialities

  • Traditional beef heart anticuchos

    The classic Peruvian skewer, grilled and shared the traditional way.

  • Chicken anticuchos

    Chicken skewers with the flavours of anticucho.

  • Anticucho Wings

    Chicken wings with the flavours of Peruvian anticuchos.

  • Grilled potatoes

    Potatoes grilled and seasoned with anticucho flavour.

What is served can vary by experience. Check the event listing before you book.

our story
