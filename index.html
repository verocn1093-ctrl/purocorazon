about us

Since 6 July 2021, Puro Corazón UK has shared Peruvian food and culinary experiences in London: traditional beef heart anticuchos, chicken anticuchos, Anticucho Wings, grilled potatoes with anticucho flavour and three sauces. Absolutely juicy, fresh ingredients, a lot of love and unique flavour. Led by director Joan Vallejos Bueno.
