about us

Puro Corazón UK shares Peruvian food and culinary experiences in London. Beef heart anticuchos, grilled potatoes, wings and sauces, made with heart and meant to be shared.

our story

Since 6 July 2021.

Puro Corazón UK promotes Peruvian culinary experiences in London, with traditional beef heart anticuchos, chicken anticuchos, Anticucho Wings, grilled potatoes with anticucho flavour and three sauces.

“Absolutely juicy, fresh ingredients, a lot of love and unique flavour.”Puro Corazón banner

Led by director Joan Vallejos Bueno.
